---
name: Quote-level offers
description: Financial sequencing and lifecycle rules for percentage and cash offers.
---

Offer eligibility is based on the full Woodwork value before GST. Both percentage and cash offers are optional, independently selectable quote-level deductions. Percentage deductions apply to the GST-inclusive total first; cash deductions follow. Neither may alter the existing category-level discount calculation, taxable value, or GST amount. The final payable amount cannot be negative.

**Why:** The financial rule explicitly requires cash discount after GST, and existing Woodwork/Accessories/Stone discount and GST behavior must remain unchanged.

**How to apply:** Keep offer calculations downstream of the normal quotation total. Save an offer's terms when a designer selects it; let them change or remove it while the project is Draft, and lock that saved selection only during the Draft-to-Generated transition.
